# Karl: 2-minute demo video script

**Setup:** Phone A = the buyer ("judge"), Phone B = the seller (teammate). Screen-record both phones plus the dashboard on a laptop. Record 6-8 AM Sunday on the seeded demo DB. Keep one take with real timing, then trim to 1:55-2:00 (voiceover is ~165 words, about 70 s of speech at a brisk pace, so the demo audio carries the rest; never exceed 2:00).

Voiceover is casual and quick. Use PLAN.md's demo script beats.

## Notes

- Keep every Karl reply on screen long enough to read (about 2 s).
- Hide real phone numbers and last names in the recording; use first names only.
- Avoid claiming anything beyond the demo: no payments, no ratings, no group chats, no tickets.
- Backup: if a live line misbehaves, re-record that beat; keep the clip as the pitch fallback video.
- Upload (unlisted or public), paste the link into Devpost, and test it in a private window.
